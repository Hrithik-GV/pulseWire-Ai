PulseWire AI — Autonomous AI Journalism & Multi-Platform Publishing Pipeline
1️⃣ UPDATED PRD (Product Requirements Document)
Product Overview

PulseWire AI is an autonomous multi-agent newsroom system that:

detects breaking/trending news
researches multiple sources
verifies information
generates articles and social content
publishes automatically to multiple platforms
tracks workflows through Omium tracing

The entire workflow runs with minimal human intervention.

Problem Statement

Journalists and content creators spend large amounts of time:

monitoring trends
collecting sources
verifying information
writing content
distributing posts

PulseWire AI automates this entire workflow.

Goal

Build a fully autonomous AI journalism pipeline that:

reacts to live news
uses multiple specialized AI agents
performs web research
generates content
publishes automatically
demonstrates async orchestration
supports Omium tracing
